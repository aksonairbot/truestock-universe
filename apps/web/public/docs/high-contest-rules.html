🏆 The reward ladder

Each rung is a new monthly active run rate — active clients we bring in during the month. Whichever rung we reach sets the reward — and your grade (A or B) decides your share of it.

🎯 Achieve more, earn more

Grade A (top achievers) earns a bigger share than Grade B (solid) at every rung. Example at 5,000 active clients on a ₹50,000 salary — Grade A earns ₹1,50,000 (3×), Grade B earns ₹1,00,000 (2×).

⚡ One-time payout each time we achieve a new monthly active run rate. Paid once for the month we hit a milestone — not a recurring or permanent raise. Every month resets to the bottom of the ladder: achieve a new monthly active run rate again to earn again.

🎖️ The two grades

Every team member is graded on their own contribution. The more you bring in, the higher your grade — and the bigger your share of the reward.

A Top performer

The strongest contributors on the team. Earn the biggest share at every milestone — up to 5× monthly salary at 10,000 active clients.

B Solid contributor

Dependable performers doing the job well. Earn the standard share at every milestone — up to 3× monthly salary at 10,000 active clients.

📋 Terms & Conditions

Please read carefully. Participation in the contest implies acceptance of these terms.

  1. Monthly & one-time. The incentive is for achieving a new monthly active run rate, calculated on the highest active-client run rate reached within a calendar month and paid once for that month. It resets at the start of each month and is not cumulative across months.
  2. Both conditions are mandatory. No incentive is payable for a month unless both qualification gates are met — cost per acquisition under ₹3,000, and at least 50% of the previous month's onboarded active clients placing a trade in the current month — regardless of the number of active clients acquired.
  3. Definition of an active client. Only accounts that are funded and have placed at least one genuine trade within 30 days of opening, with valid KYC and no mis-selling or compliance flag, are counted. Self, family, or related-party accounts do not count.
  4. Discretionary & variable. This is a discretionary, variable, one-time payment. It does not form part of fixed salary or CTC, creates no entitlement to any future payout, and does not set a precedent.
  5. Eligibility. This contest is open to team members only — managers are excluded and are not eligible for any payout under it. Otherwise payable only to employees who are in active service on the payout date and not serving notice, absconding, or under disciplinary action. Employees who have resigned or been separated before payout are not eligible.
  6. Grading. Assignment of Grade A or Grade B is based on management's assessment of individual contribution and is at management's sole discretion.
  7. Verification & clawback. All figures are as per the company's official CRM and records. Any incentive linked to accounts later found fraudulent, mis-sold, duplicated, or reversed may be recovered or adjusted against future payouts.
  8. Taxes. All payouts are subject to applicable taxes and statutory deductions.
  9. Changes to the scheme. Management reserves the right to amend, pause, or withdraw the contest — including milestones, multiples, conditions, and grade definitions — at any time, with reasonable notice.
  10. Management decision is final. In all matters of interpretation, eligibility, grading, calculation, and any dispute arising from this contest, the decision of management shall be final and binding.
