01

How the waitlist works (FOMO Marketing)

Every ad leads to the HIGH waitlist. Waitlist mechanics, the right audience, strong creatives and the platform mix together bring our cost to ₹30 per signup — against the ₹80–150 typical for this category. At the centre is FOMO: limited seats, a live public counter and capped founding status make joining feel urgent, and that urgency is what converts.

1
One field. A phone number.No email, no KYC, no password. One of the lowest-friction asks in Indian fintech — it keeps the cost per signup down.
2
Seats are limited, and the counter is publicFOMO20,062 of 25,000 seats claimed, shown live on the page. Scarcity makes the same ad traffic convert better — at no extra cost.
3
Then we ask for an emailOnly after registration, so it never costs a signup. Those who share their email are the most invested — our priority list for launch, founding-member outreach and day-one activation.
4
The first 1,000 become Founding MembersFOMONot a discount — status. Founding Member access is capped at 1,000 for good, however large the list grows.
5
Share to climb the listFOMOEvery friend who joins on your link climbs you toward a founding seat — and sharers unlock early access to the app. Referred signups cost about ₹15 against the ₹30 average.
6
The waitlist stays live until 1 lakhSeats expand as we scale; founding access never does. The closer the list gets to 1 lakh, the harder people push to climb.

Verified signup — phone number confirmed by OTP on the waitlist.  ·  Created user — account opened and KYC completed.  ·  Funded user — money added to the account. Many fund first and place their first trade in the following weeks, so funded runs ahead of traded.  ·  Traded user = active user — has placed at least one trade. This is the primary goal of the programme. Unit costs are set at the base of each range.
